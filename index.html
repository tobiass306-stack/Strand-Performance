THE 2026 REALITY

Cold email is broken. Most agencies won't tell you.

Google and Microsoft tightened spam filters severely in 2025. Industry-average B2B reply rates crashed from 5–8% to 1–3%. Most cold email agencies still use tactics from 2022 — and their clients pay for empty pipelines.

PROBLEM 01

Emails sent from your main domain

One spam flag and your entire business email starts landing in junk. Amateur setups burn the very domain you use for real clients.

PROBLEM 02

Copy that reads like a bot wrote it

Mail-merge templates without real personalisation are spotted in the first line. Prospects delete them in 2 seconds. 60% of "cold email" is functionally spam.

PROBLEM 03

English-only in Nordic markets

95% of cold email services send English only. Native Swedish/Norwegian/German copy gets 2–3x higher engagement (Litmus 2024 data). Most agencies leave this on the table.

PROBLEM 04

No warmup, no infrastructure

Sending 1,000 emails from a fresh domain = instant blocklist. Proper warmup takes 14 days per domain. Skipped by most quick-turnaround services.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Is cold email legal in the Nordics? +
Yes, for B2B under GDPR's "legitimate interest" basis, provided your email is relevant, personalised, and includes clear identification and easy opt-out. We handle all of this by default.
How is this different from typical AAA / cold email agencies? +
Three ways: (1) Native Nordic language — most send English only. (2) Sub-60-second personalisation per lead, not mail-merge. (3) Dedicated infrastructure per client — many services reuse the same warm inboxes across all clients, which hurts deliverability. Also: one founder, direct access.
What kind of results can I actually expect? +
Realistic targets: 35–55% open rate, 3–8% reply rate, 8–15% positive reply rate on the best-performing segments. From that, 5–15 booked meetings per month depending on your ICP and offer clarity. These are targets — not guarantees. Every ICP is different.
Can you show me case studies? +
Honest answer: Strand Outreach is new. We haven't collected client case studies yet — which is exactly why the first 3 clients get 50% off in exchange for a testimonial. On your strategy call, I'll show you the exact numbers from running Strand Outreach on ourselves. Real data, not fabricated screenshots.
What if it doesn't work for us? +
Cold email performance is inherently variable — it depends on your ICP quality, offer clarity, and market timing. Every month, we report on real numbers: open rates, replies, meetings booked, and what's working vs. not. If a segment isn't converting, we adjust: new subject lines, new copy angles, new prospect segments. We don't guarantee outcomes, but we do guarantee transparent execution and constant iteration until we find what lands.
Do you handle the meetings, or do we? +
You handle the meetings. That's the whole point — we get warm prospects to your calendar so your sales team (or you) can close them. We deliver leads, not closes. Strand does not act as your sales rep.
Which industries work best? +
B2B service companies with clear deal size and mid-sized teams: SaaS (ACV €5–50K), management consulting (engagements €15–100K), software dev agencies (projects €20–500K), specialist recruiters, cybersecurity consultancies. Company size: 15–40 employees works best.
Can I just buy the setup and run it myself? +
Yes — €2,500 flat fee, delivered in 5–7 days. You get the complete infrastructure and SOP documentation. Ideal if you have someone in-house who can run outbound but doesn't want to build from scratch.
